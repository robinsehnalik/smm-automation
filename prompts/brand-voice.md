# Brand Voice & Social Media Copywriting Guidelines

This document defines the tone of voice, formatting principles, and platform-specific constraints for the automated variant generator (`SMM-POST-01`).

---

## 1. Core Brand Tone

* **Authoritative & Direct**: Communicate with clarity, confidence, and precision. Avoid filler, buzzwords, and vague hype.
* **Modern & Professional**: Maintain an accessible, polished, and contemporary voice. Speak peer-to-peer with our audience.
* **Value-First Structure**: Always lead with the most compelling insight, problem, or takeaway before elaborating.
* **Grounded & Factual**: Only make claims that are supported by the provided key message. Never invent product features, pricing, or unverified performance metrics.

---

## 2. Platform Style Specifications

### LinkedIn
* **Target Audience**: Business leaders, industry practitioners, and decision-makers.
* **Structure**:
  * Strong 1–2 line opening hook (visible before the "see more" cutoff).
  * 2–3 short paragraphs or clean bullet points separated by generous whitespace.
  * Clear, professional call-to-action (e.g., asking for perspectives or linking to a resource).
* **Length**: 800–1,500 characters recommended (hard technical limit: 3,000 characters).
* **Hashtags**: 3–5 focused industry hashtags placed at the very end of the post.

### X (formerly Twitter)
* **Target Audience**: Fast-scrolling readers seeking punchy takeaways and immediate value.
* **Structure**:
  * Immediate hook packed into the first sentence.
  * Direct takeaway or perspective.
* **Length**: Strictly within 280 weighted characters (URLs consume a fixed 23 characters; emojis and double-byte characters count as 2). Target 200–250 characters for clean readability.
* **Hashtags**: 1–2 relevant hashtags maximum, either integrated naturally into the sentence or placed at the end.

### Instagram
* **Target Audience**: Visual consumers seeking engaging storytelling and community connection.
* **Structure**:
  * Engaging narrative hook that complements the visual asset.
  * Explanatory body copy formatted with line breaks for readability.
  * Clear call-to-action (e.g., "Tap the link in bio to learn more" or "Save this post for later").
* **Media Requirement**: **Strictly requires at least one image or video URL.** Text-only posts are rejected by the Instagram Graph API.
* **Length**: 300–800 characters recommended (hard limit: 2,200 characters).
* **Hashtags**: 8–15 targeted hashtags separated from the caption by line breaks (maximum allowed by platform: 30).

---

## 3. Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| :--- | :--- |
| Lead with actionable insights or compelling questions | Use generic engagement bait ("Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Tag a friend") |
| Format with deliberate whitespace and short paragraphs | Present unbroken walls of text |
| Use clear punctuation and standard capitalization | Overuse emojis or substitute emojis for bullet points |
| Adapt copy rhythm and vocabulary to each platform | Cross-post identical copy across different networks |
| Verify that Instagram posts always include media links | Attempt to publish text-only updates to Instagram |
